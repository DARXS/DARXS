## Experience

### Software Quality Analyst · ACT Digital @ Stellantis
`Dec 2025 → Present`

- **Diagnostics:** field-failure investigation on multicore ECUs (Infotainment, IPC, TBM) through bench, HIL and in-vehicle testing.
- **OTA and network health:** UDS diagnostics, FOTA/AOTA/MOTA campaign sign-off, rollback validation, CAN/LIN health analysis.
- **Peripheral Systems:** warranty and field-quality analysis (3MIS/12MIS) on acoustic and RF modules, with RCA/8D, PPM/FTY tracking and Power BI KPIs.
- **Tools:** Vector CANalyzer, CANcase, CANdela, DIAglyser, DIAnalyser, CDA.

### Maintenance Planning, Quality & Reliability Intern · Stellantis South America
`Nov 2024 → Nov 2025`

- Combined SAP PM operations with maintenance data governance, standardizing KPIs (MTTR, MTBF, availability, maintenance cost) and tracking emergency work orders.
- Built Power BI dashboards integrating SAP PM and Excel, with ETL in Power Query and DAX.
- Applied RCA, 5 Whys, Ishikawa and FTA to find recurring shop-floor failure patterns, supporting decisions to reduce unplanned stops.

### Embedded Systems Coordinator · Mangue Baja UFPE
`Apr 2024 → Apr 2025`

- Led the electronics division across the full E/E lifecycle, from requirements to on-track validation.
- Ran the team with V-Model, Scrum and Kanban: backlog, sprint priorities and acceptance criteria.
- Directed C/C++ firmware integration and low-level driver development.
- Led RCA investigations (8D, 5 Whys, Ishikawa, FTA) on systemic hardware and software failures.
- Aligned electronics integration with Powertrain, Brakes, Suspension/Steering and Design/Ergonomics.

### Embedded Systems Developer · Mangue Baja UFPE
`May 2023 → Apr 2025`

- Designed, assembled and validated the prototype's ECUs end to end.
- Wrote C/C++ firmware for ESP32 and STM32: drivers, interrupts, timers, state machines, circular buffers.
- Configured UART, SPI, I2C and the CAN network, and routed the wiring harness.
- Used FreeRTOS for task scheduling and inter-task communication.
- Ran bench and in-vehicle tests with oscilloscopes and multimeters.
